# Dev Deepawali 2026 in Varanasi: Date, Ghats, Parking & Getting There

**Dev Deepawali 2026 falls on Tuesday, 24 November, the full moon (Kartik Purnima) about sixteen days after Diwali.** The lamps are lit along all 80+ ghats of Varanasi soon after sunset, around 5 PM. Roads near the ghats are closed to vehicles from late morning, so the trick is to park on the edge of the old city early and walk in, or watch from a boat.

This guide covers the date and timings, the best places to watch, what to expect from road closures, where to park, and how to plan the day.

## Dev Deepawali 2026 at a Glance

| What | Details |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Tuesday, 24 November 2026 |
| **Purnima tithi** | About 11:42 PM on 23 Nov to 8:23 PM on 24 Nov (IST) |
| **Lamps lit** | After sunset, around 5:00-5:15 PM |
| **Main events** | Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh, diyas on every ghat, laser show at Chet Singh Ghat, fireworks |
| **Crowd level** | Very high, among the busiest nights of the year |
| **Vehicles** | Banned near the ghats from late morning (see below) |

Tithi timings can shift by a few minutes between panchangs. Check a local panchang closer to the date if exact timing matters for your puja.

## What Is Dev Deepawali?

Dev Deepawali means "the Diwali of the gods". It is believed that the gods descend to bathe in the Ganga on Kartik Purnima to celebrate Lord Shiva's victory over the demon Tripurasura, which is why the day is also called Tripurari Purnima. Lakhs of clay diyas are lit on the ghat steps, and the whole riverfront turns gold.

The night also closes Ganga Mahotsav, a cultural festival of classical music and dance on the ghats in the days before Kartik Purnima. Expect it to run roughly 21-24 November in 2026, though the official schedule had not been announced when this guide was written.

## Best Places to Watch

**From a boat (best view, book early).** The full sweep of lit ghats is only visible from the river. Shared and private boats run for about 3 hours, usually from Dashashwamedh or Assi. Prices rise sharply for this one night. Operators list shared seats from roughly ₹3,500-4,000 per person for 2026, and boats sell out weeks ahead. Check whether food, life jackets and GST are included before paying.

**Dashashwamedh and Rajendra Prasad Ghat.** The grand Ganga Aarti happens here. It is the most spectacular spot and also the most crowded. Arrive by early afternoon if you want to stand anywhere near the front.

**Chet Singh Ghat.** The laser and projection show is staged here. It is best seen from the water, but the ghats on either side give a good angle.

**Assi Ghat and the southern ghats.** These are a little less packed than Dashashwamedh while still beautifully lit. Assi is also the easiest ghat to reach from the BHU and Lanka side of the city.

**Namo Ghat and Raj Ghat (north end).** These are newer, open ghats with more space. They are a good choice if you are coming from the Cantt or Kashi station side and want to avoid the central crush.

## Road Closures: What to Expect

The Varanasi traffic police issue the official advisory only 2-3 days before the festival, so the exact 2026 rules are not out yet. In past years the pattern has been consistent:

- **No-vehicle zone around the central ghats.** In 2022 the area between Godowlia and Dashashwamedh was closed to vehicles, with diversions starting around 11 AM. Later years added no-entry zones near Assi and the other main ghats.
- **Autos and e-rickshaws restricted.** Three-wheelers have been stopped from entering the core zone from around midday until late evening.
- **Designated parking on the edges.** Past advisories and guides list parking at Maidagin (Town Hall), Beniabagh, Luxa, Godowlia multi-storey (until it fills or is closed) and the Sigra/Maldahiya side.

**Plan for this:** you will walk the last 1-2 km to any central ghat, whatever vehicle you come in. We will update this guide when the 2026 advisory is published.

## How to Get There

| Option | Good | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| **Scooty or bike** | Reach edge parking early, skip the evening surge, leave the moment the crowd thins | Parking fills by afternoon; no entry into the core zone |
| **Cab** | Comfortable from the airport or station | Surge pricing, long waits, and drop-offs far from the ghats |
| **Auto / e-rickshaw** | Cheap for short hops | Restricted near the ghats; hard to find after the event |
| **Walking** | The only way into the ghat area itself | Plan 1-2 km each way, through dense crowds |

A two-wheeler will not get you onto the ghats on this night. Nothing will. What it does is solve the hard part, which is getting into and out of the old city without waiting for a cab at 10 PM along with a few lakh other people.

### A practical plan with a scooty

1. **Pick up your scooty the day before**, or early on the 24th, from a hub away from the old city, such as [Sigra](/locations/sigra), [Mahmoorganj](/locations/mahmoorganj) or [Lanka](/locations/lanka). Our [Godowlia hub](/locations/godowlia) sits inside the usual no-vehicle zone, so don't plan a same-day pickup there.
2. **Ride in by 1-2 PM** and park at one of the edge parking areas. Maidagin and Beniabagh suit Dashashwamedh and the central ghats. The Lanka/BHU side suits Assi.
3. **Walk to your ghat** and claim a spot. Carry water and a light shawl, because November evenings on the river get cold.
4. **After the aarti and fireworks, wait 30-45 minutes** for the first wave of the crowd to clear, then walk back to your vehicle.
5. **Ride out away from the river.** Traffic eases quickly once you are past Godowlia and Maidagin.

Scooty rentals start at ₹299/day ([see all rates](/pricing)), with a sanitised helmet included. On a night when cabs surge, that usually costs less than one round trip.

## If You Are Staying Several Days

Hotels near the ghats sell out and get expensive around Dev Deepawali, so many visitors stay in Sigra, Mahmoorganj or near BHU instead. With your own scooty, the days around the festival are worth using:

- **Morning boat ride and ghat walk** on the 23rd or 25th, when the lamps are fewer but the crowds are gone.
- **Sarnath day trip.** It is 10 km away and calm compared with the city. See our [Assi Ghat to Sarnath route guide](/blog/assi-ghat-to-sarnath-route).
- **Ganga Mahotsav evenings** in the days leading up to the full moon.
- **Ramnagar Fort** across the river, a short ride from Assi and Lanka.

## Safety Tips for the Night

- **Ride sober, wear the helmet, and keep your speed down.** Roads near the ghats are packed with pedestrians and police barricades.
- **Note where you parked.** Drop a pin, because every lane looks the same at 10 PM.
- **Keep valuables zipped and in front of you.** The crowds are dense.
- **Agree on a meeting point** with your group in case phones lose signal, which often happens in the crowd.
- **Don't fly a drone.** In past years Varanasi has been declared a no-fly zone for the festival days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When is Dev Deepawali in 2026?**
A: Dev Deepawali 2026 is on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, on Kartik Purnima. The diyas are lit on the ghats after sunset, from around 5 PM.

**Q: Can I take my scooty or car to the ghats on Dev Deepawali?**
A: No. In past years the roads around the central ghats, including Godowlia to Dashashwamedh, have been closed to vehicles from late morning. Park at designated areas such as Maidagin, Beniabagh or the Sigra side and walk the last 1-2 km.

**Q: Which is the best ghat to watch Dev Deepawali?**
A: Dashashwamedh Ghat has the grand Ganga Aarti but is the most crowded. Assi Ghat and Namo Ghat are less crowded alternatives. The best overall view is from a boat on the river, which needs advance booking.

**Q: How much does a Dev Deepawali boat ride cost?**
A: Prices rise sharply for this one night. Operators list shared boat seats from roughly ₹3,500 to ₹4,000 per person for 2026, and private boats cost more. Book a few weeks ahead and check what is included.

**Q: What time should I reach the ghats on Dev Deepawali?**
A: Reach your parking spot by 1-2 PM and the ghats by mid-afternoon. Vehicle restrictions usually begin before noon, and the best spots fill up hours before the lamps are lit.

**Q: Is it worth renting a scooty for Dev Deepawali?**
A: Yes, if you are staying away from the ghats. A scooty gets you to the edge parking early and out quickly afterwards, when cabs surge and autos are hard to find. It cannot enter the no-vehicle zone, so expect to walk the final stretch.
